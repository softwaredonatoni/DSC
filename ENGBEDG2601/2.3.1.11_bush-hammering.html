2.3.1.11 BUSH-HAMMERING

    This operation allows roughening a part of the upper surface of the piece.

    Parameters

    NOTE

    This working can only be performed in non-through mode.

    Distance from edge
    Specify the distance of the chamfering from the edge of the piece.

    Start distance maximum speed
    Range in which the belt will move at reduced speed to facilitate the entry of the mandrel into the piece.
    Space between the start of the work and the point where the work is performed at full speed.

    Start distance maximum speed
    Range in which the belt will move at reduced speed to facilitate the removal of the mandrel from the piece.
    Space between the point where the processing ends full operation and the end of the piece.

    Description

    The saw blade thermal alarm is generated when the inverter controlling spindle rotation malfunctions.

    Symptoms

    …..

    Possible causes

    The causes of this error must be assessed case by case, because the electrical cabinet must be opened and the inverter error messages checked.

    NOTE: to open the electrical cabinet, follow the procedure 

    Opening the live electrical cabinet

    Messaggi errore Inverter.pdf

    Possible solutions

    The solutions will depend on the inverter status. It is important to determine whether the problem is caused by connections downstream or upstream of the inverter:

    • For example, disconnect the spindle cable, try to start the saw blade, and check whether the error occurs again.

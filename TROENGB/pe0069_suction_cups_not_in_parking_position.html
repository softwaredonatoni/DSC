Technical troubleshooting sheet Emergency diagnostic procedure

    PE0069SUCTION CUPS NOT IN PARKING POSITION

    Before proceeding, carefully read the

    SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

    DescriptionSuction cups out of parking position

    SeverityLow01 / 05

    Authorized personnelMachine operator

    Required PPE:

    Description

    This error appears when the machine detects that one or both suction cups are not in the parking position.

    Symptoms

    …..

    Possible causes

    1. Air supply missing

    2. Suction cups lowered

    3. Sensors incorrectly adjusted

    4. Faulty sensors

    Possible solutions

    1. If this alarm is accompanied by PE0066 Air supply missing, check that air is available at the machine

    2. Press the dedicated button to raise the suction cups

    3. Open the steel covers on the sides of the suction-cup arms and check that the sensors at the top of the cylinders are on when the suction cups are raised.

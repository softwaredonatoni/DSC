Technical troubleshooting sheet Emergency diagnostic procedure

    PE0021TILTING WORKTABLE NOT IN POSITION

    Before proceeding, carefully read the

    SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

    DescriptionTilting worktable out of position

    SeverityModerate02 / 05

    Authorized personnelMachine operator

    Required PPE:

    Description

    The tilting worktable is outside its parking position.

    Symptoms

    …..

    Possible causes

    This alarm may have several causes, either mechanical or electrical:

    The machine detects that the worktable is not in the parking position. Possible reasons include:

    1. The worktable is tilted and does not actuate the limit switch;

    2. The worktable is in the parking position but does not actuate the limit switch;

    3. Faulty worktable limit switch;

    4. Fault in the connection between the worktable limit switch and the electrical cabinet.

    5. The worktable cannot be returned to the parking position because of a mechanical problem, such as broken hoses, insufficient oil, or a faulty pump

    Possible solutions

    WARNING!

    When the alarm is active, the machine cannot be moved. Therefore, if the machine is not in the parking position, it will be locked. In Parametrix, open the first "Padlock", press the "Spanner_Screwdriver" button, and disable the worktable. (For older Parametrix versions, open KvIsoGui.exe, access the restricted area using the password "esagv", and disable the "Worktable" option.)


    After ensuring that the machine is in the parking position, perform the following procedures:

    1. Use the dedicated selector on the console to move the worktable to the parking position;

    2. Physically check whether the worktable actuates the sensor when in the parking position. If it does not, check whether the worktable rests against the stop bolts; if so, adjust the sensor so that it is actuated by the worktable;

    3. Open KvIsoGui.exe, press the "Menu" button, open "I/O Monitor", and verify that the corresponding input changes when the limit switch is pressed. (FC_SUPP_LOW   AT %IW4.13; * IW4.13 lower worktable limit switch)

      1. If the input does not change state, use a tester to check whether the limit switch operates correctly (for example, place the probes on the NO terminals; pressing the limit switch must close the contact)

      2. If the limit switch operates correctly, check the connections by following the

        Electrical diagram

    4. Check pump operation and verify that the system is not faulty;

    NOTE: Remember to re-enable the worktable

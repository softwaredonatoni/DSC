Technical troubleshooting sheet Emergency diagnostic procedure

    PE0065VACUUM PUMP THERMAL SWITCH ACTIVE

    Before proceeding, carefully read the

    SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

    DescriptionVacuum pump thermal switch active

    SeverityModerate02 / 05

    Authorized personnelMachine operator

    Required PPE:

    Description

    Fault in the worktable lifting system; the circuit breaker for the vacuum pump has tripped. It protects the system against both short circuits and overloads. To reset the circuit breaker, move the selector to 0 and then to 1;

    Symptoms

    …..

    Possible causes

    The causes can be divided into Mechanical or Electrical problems

    Mechanical problems:

    1. Vacuum pump motor seized; try starting the vacuum pump and check whether it actually turns.

    2. The vacuum pump tank is empty

    Electrical problems:

    1. Short circuit between motor phases;

    2. One phase missing;

    3. Short circuit in the motor windings;

    4. Faulty circuit breaker or current threshold set to an incorrect value


    Possible solutions

    MECHANICAL

    1. First, check that the motor cooling fan rotates freely; for example, use a screwdriver to try to move the fan. If it is jammed, it is necessary to

    2. fill the tank up to the overflow level


    ELECTRICAL

    WARNING!

    Assistance from an electrician or a Donatoni technician on site is recommended

    1. Check that there is no continuity between the various phases. Disconnect the cable from the pump electrical box, insulate the terminals, reset the circuit breaker, and activate the oil pump. If the circuit breaker trips, the cable may be the cause;

    2. Measure the input voltage with a multimeter and verify that all phases are present. If, for example, one phase is missing, check that all terminals are tight;

    3. If the circuit breaker does not trip during the test in step 1, connect the cable to the pump. If the circuit breaker trips when the pump is started, the pump is faulty and must be replaced;

    4. If the circuit breaker continues to trip after the previous tests, the circuit breaker itself may be faulty. Also verify that the threshold is set to 4A

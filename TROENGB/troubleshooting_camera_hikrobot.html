TROUBLESHOOTING CAMERA HIKROBOT

    Page dedicated to troubleshooting issues related to Hikrobot cameras.

    WARNING: try only AFTER confirming that issues are actually related to Camera and not to TWAIN.

    Base (to be reviewed as it was simply copied from "Matrix Vision")

    Ensure that for any issue, verify that the camera is properly connected without connection problems. To do this open the program mvIPConfigure.

    1. In the devices list must be displayed the camera; otherwise it is not connected to power or network.

    2. If I click on the camera, it must result that there are no connection issues. In case problems are detected, an automatic troubleshooting procedure is usually proposed.

    3. On the right, a series of parameters are displayed, including the assigned ip and negotiated connection speed. The latter MUST be 1000 mb (or 1 gb). If less is negotiated (e.g., 100mb), there’s likely an issue with one of the two ethernet cables (camera ↔ router or pc ↔ router). Test with a crossover cable to see if replacing either resolves the problem.

    Symptom

    It has become difficult to take snapshots or even just get a shot

    Possible cause

    The repeated connection and disconnection of the device caused some unwanted parameter saving.

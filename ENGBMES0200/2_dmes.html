    DOC2 DMES

    Donatoni MES (DMES) software provides simplified production management, up to a complete version for managing the company's production stages.

    DMES CORE

    DONATONI DMES CORE is the basic package of the DMES system, designed to provide complete, simple and centralised control of the production process from the outset.
    It is the ideal solution for starting to digitalise your factory, with practical tools to improve organisation, traceability and productivity.

    DMES TRACE

    DONATONI DMES TRACE is the complete package for digital production management, integrating all basic functions and adding advanced tools for label customisation and printing.
    A solution designed to provide total production control and precise, professional traceability of workpieces, from project to shipment.

    DMES MATRIX

    DONATONI DMES MATRIX is an advanced platform that combines all the functions of DMES TRACE with a powerful operating module dedicated to slab management.
    It is the ideal solution for those who want total control over the entire production cycle, from planning and workpiece labelling through to organising the slabs to be processed.

    DMES PRO

    DONATONI DMES PRO is the most complete and structured package in the entire DMES suite. It combines all the functions of the MATRIX package in a single platform, enhancing them with advanced tools for managing jobs, work cycles and the company's entire production process.

    With DMES PRO, you do more than monitor machines or manage slabs: you have an integrated system to plan, control, assign, track and document every stage of the work, from order receipt to delivery of the finished workpiece. It is designed for companies that want to digitalise and optimise every link in the production chain, increasing efficiency and improving customer service.

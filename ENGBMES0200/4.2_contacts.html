    DOC4.2 CONTACTS

    This MES page allows customer and supplier records to be managed. From here, the user can access the dedicated areas to create, view or update the main information.

    Buttons

    Customers
    Select this button to open the section dedicated to customers. In this area, the information of customers registered in the MES can be managed.

    Suppliers
    Select this button to open the section dedicated to suppliers. In this area, the information of suppliers registered in the MES can be managed.

    DOC3.15.3 EDITING A COMMERCIAL PIECE

    A small panel opens in which the dimensions of a commercial piece can be changed.

    Buttons

    Dimension magnet
    Resizes a piece to the same dimensions as the adjacent piece by dragging one side.

    Width
    Real-time piece width.

    Height
    Real-time piece height.

    Extension amount
    Number of mm by which the piece is to be extended.

    Apply extension
    Button for applying the piece extension.

    Touch step
    The piece can also be extended by dragging one of its sides; the touch step is the drag increment.
    If the piece is dragged slightly, it is extended by 5mm; if the touch step is increased to 20, the piece is extended by 20mm at each step.

    Executing the operations

    Extending the piece

    Apply an extension to one side of the piece.

    Enter 1000 in the dedicated box, press the side of the piece, then press the button with the right arrow.

    Touch step

    Use Touch Step to extend the piece by 500mm. When the side is dragged, it is extended by 500mm per step.

    Enter 500 in the dedicated box, press the side of the piece and drag it to the right.

    Resizing

    Resize a piece by dragging its side towards the side of another piece.

    Click the side of the piece to be resized and drag it towards the side of the other reference piece.

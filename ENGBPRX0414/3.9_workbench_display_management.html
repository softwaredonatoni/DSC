    DOC3.9 WORKBENCH DISPLAY MANAGEMENT

    This function opens a small panel with tools for resizing the work area.

    Buttons

    Ruler
    Measures the distance between two points in the work area.

    Zoom _
    Increases the size of the work area display.

    Zoom -
    Decreases the size of the work area display.

    Reset zoom
    Restores the original size of the work area.

    Window zoom
    Applies zoom to a selected portion of the work area.

    Pan view
    Moves the work area together with all pieces on it.

    Show / Hide background color
    Enables or disables the background color in the work area.

    Show / Hide piece names
    Enables or disables display of piece names in the work area.

    Show / Hide right angles
    Enables or disables display of right angles of pieces in the work area.

    Ruler

    To use this function, the operator must indicate two points in the work area. They are represented by two yellow crosses.

    A small panel appears below the ruler button showing the distance between the two points.

    Horizontal

    Horizontal distance between the first and second points.

    Vertical

    Vertical distance between the first and second points.

    Total

    Total distance between the first and second points.

    X

    Real-time horizontal mouse position.

    Y

    Real-time vertical mouse position.

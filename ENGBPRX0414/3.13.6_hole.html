    DOC3.13.6 HOLE

    This page can be used to create a single hole at a specific position or a series of holes at a fixed distance.

    Machining types

    To use this machining operation, first choose one of the two available modes by selecting the corresponding button:

    NOTE

    The 'Hole in the material' and 'Remove Disk's Material' modes are mutually exclusive: enabling one automatically disables the other.

    Hole in the material
    The operator can create holes based on coordinates.

    Remove Disk's Material
    Adds holes to the piece to remove material left by the disk.

    Hole in the material

    When this machining mode is selected, the currently selected vertex is highlighted in orange and a preview of the hole to be added is projected from it, also shown in orange and generated according to the set parameters.

    Parameters

    Penetration

    Indicates the depth to be reached by the machining operation.

    Distance X axis

    Distance along the X axis from the starting point of the selected cut at which the hole is created.

    Distance Y axis

    Distance along the Y axis from the starting point of the selected cut at which the hole is created.

    NOTE

    Relative to the selected point, the area to the right of the circle is considered positive for X and the area above the circle positive for Y, as shown in the figure.

    Executing Hole in the material

    NOTE

    The machining operation can be applied by pressing the button to the left of the appropriate cut in the polygon list.
    A red icon with an 'X' indicates that the machining operation cannot be applied to the desired cut.

    Once a vertex has been selected and the distance and penetration parameters set, the machining operation can be confirmed by pressing the green OK button.

    Each hole successfully inserted in the material updates the preview and is automatically inserted in the 'Added Machining Operations' section on the left:

    NOTE

    Unlike Remove Disk's Material, however, the center points of arcs can also be selected; these are highlighted in the preview together with all other valid reference points for inserting holes in the material.

    Remove Disk's Material

    When this mode is enabled, the selected vertex becomes the origin point of an orange preview consisting of multiple holes; their size depends on the mounted core drill and their number is determined by the Hole distance parameter.

    Remove Disk's Material parameters

    Penetration

    Indicates the depth to be reached by the machining operation.

    Automatic holes distance

    Determines the distance between the hole origin points.

    Executing Remove Disk's Material

    NOTE

    The machining operation can be applied by pressing the button to the left of the appropriate cut in the polygon list.
    A red icon with an 'X' indicates that the machining operation cannot be applied to the desired cut.

    Remove Disk's Material mode can be used between two consecutive cuts, regardless of whether both are straight lines, both curves, or one of each type.

    The hole is added to the internal-polygon list and identifies the disk-material removal machining operation.

    Buttons available in Remove Disk's Material mode

    Concave holes
    Applies three holes to each concave corner.

    Holes number
    Allows the number of holes to be inserted to be selected.

    DOC3.13.10 DOWNSTAND

    This function creates a rectangular piece from the side of an object to form a downstand.

    NOTE

    This machining operation is available only for straight cuts.

    Machining types

    To use this machining operation, first select one of the two available modes by pressing the corresponding button:

    Backsplash mode
    Creates rectangular pieces with all straight sides.

    Downstand mode
    In this mode, the two sides to be joined are inclined at the same angle.

    Parameters

    Height

    Height of the downstand inserted when the machining operation is executed.
    The width is determined in advance by the selected cut.

    Miter piece sides

    When enabled, automatically inserts miter cuts where cuts would otherwise collide.

    Additional Downstand mode parameter

    Degrees

    This parameter is active only when Backsplash mode is selected.
    It specifies the angle at which the machining operation is applied.

    Executing the machining operation

    NOTE

    The machining operation can be applied by pressing the button to the left of the appropriate cut in the polygon list.
    A red icon with an 'X' indicates that the machining operation cannot be applied to the required cut.

    Clicking directly on the cut in the preview, or on the icon to the left of the cut in the corresponding list on the left where available, executes the selected machining operation.
    Whenever a downstand or fillet is successfully applied to the material, the preview and the cut list on the left are updated.

    Each piece created using this function is added to the Parametrix piece list.
    The new pieces are also added to a list located immediately above the preview in the ‘Edit Piece’ panel;

    This list can be used to select the required piece in the block and display its preview and cuts in the cut list.

    Executing Downstand

    The following examples show the application of two downstands with the “Miter piece sides“ parameter enabled or disabled.
    First, a downstand 50 mm high is applied to the lower side of the rectangle, followed by a second downstand on its right side.
    In this mode, the original piece (Piece) and any generated pieces (Piece_1 and Piece_2) automatically receive miter cuts on all mutually adjacent sides.
    The miter angle is determined by the degrees parameter.

    Miter piece sides disabled

    In this case, the adjacent sides between the original piece and the newly created pieces are automatically replaced with miter cuts.
    The cut inclination depends on the set Degrees parameter.

    Miter piece sides enabled

    As in the previous case, miter cuts are inserted on sides adjacent to the various pieces, and their inclination depends on the value of the Degrees parameter.
    In addition, when the “Miter piece sides” parameter is enabled, as with backsplashes, if the new pieces have aligned vertices, the sides sharing a vertex that are not adjacent to the original piece are automatically replaced with miter cuts.

    Splitting pieces

    When one or more pieces have been associated with the selected piece through Downstand or Backsplash operations, a dedicated button for managing the associated pieces appears at the top of the “Edit Piece” panel.

    Split pieces
    Separates downstands and backsplashes from the original piece.

    Before splitting, the associated pieces are managed as a single unit within the work area. In this state, they form a block of multiple elements that undergo the same operations and have not yet been separated.

    For example, when the original piece, a downstand or a backsplash is added to the work area from the piece list, all other associated elements are also inserted automatically.

    When the Split Pieces button is pressed, each piece generated using a downstand or backsplash is disassociated from the original piece and treated as an independent item.

    The associated-piece list previously shown above the preview in the Edit Piece panel and the Split Pieces button then disappear from the interface.

    NOTE

    When the pieces are split, each piece retains the miter cuts created during downstand or backsplash generation, together with all customisations and changes made while it was still associated with the original piece.

    DOC3.21.6 WASHING CYCLE

    A washing cycle for the area can be started manually, automatic addition of the entire workbench can be enabled, and a portion to be cleaned can be selected.

    Parameters

    Width
    Displays the width of the area being created.

    height
    Displays the height of the area being created.

    Create area
    Creates an area that the operator can resize and move according to where washing is to be performed.

    Move
    The operator can move the area being created.

    Piece washing
    Creates a washing area slightly larger than a selected piece.

    Clean workbench
    The entire workbench area will undergo a washing cycle.

    Automatic washing cycle
    Automatically adds a “Bench Washing” phase at the end of the cut list.
    It will be added when switching to the cutting window.

    Washing-cycle insertion mode

    Adding a washing cycle inserts a rectangle in the work area indicating the portion of the workbench to be washed.
    Multiple washing cycles can be inserted; their areas can also overlap partially or completely.
    Each new washing cycle is added to the end of the cut list on the right.

    Manual area selection

    Pressing the button enables custom washing areas to be added.
    The area can then be moved and resized.

    After adjusting the washing-cycle area as desired, another area to be washed can be added by pressing the button again.

    DOC2.1 MANUAL (SUCTION CUPS)

    The suction cups can be operated manually to move pieces from one work area to another.

    Suction cup buttons

    Enable/disable suction cups
    The screen shows a top view of the suction cup assembly divided into its different zones. Pressing a zone changes its status.
    Green indicates that the zone is configured to generate a vacuum.
    Red indicates that the zone is configured for blowing.

    SUCTION CUPS: RAISE - Park suction cups
    Pressing the button retracts the suction cups to their rest/parking position.

    SUCTION CUPS: LOWER - Prepare suction cups
    Pressing the button lowers the suction cups from the parking position to the limit stop. Make sure there is enough space for the suction cups to extend fully.

    LIFT PIECE: RELEASE - Unload piece
    Pressing the button lowers the Z axis until the material contacts the work surface, switches off the vacuum pump and activates blowing from all suction cups to release the piece. After a timeout, the axis is raised to the safety position.

    LIFT PIECE: START - Lift piece
    This control brings the suction cups into contact with the material, activates the vacuum pump in the areas marked green and blowing in the areas marked red. When the vacuum switch indicates that vacuum has been generated, the axis is raised to the position set for movements.

    WARNING!

    Pay attention to the active suction cups and use the largest possible area to lift the piece.

    BLOW - Blowing control
    The button switches blowing on or off from the suction cups that are not set for vacuum. The indicator shows whether blowing is on (green) or off (red).

    VACUUM - Vacuum pump control
    This button switches the vacuum pump on and off. The indicator shows whether the pump is on (green) or off (red).

    Safety conditions

    Pay particular attention to the active suction cups during lifting, making sure that the areas used are sufficient to lift the piece being moved. Always try to use the largest available vacuum area to grip the piece and check that the material has no defects or breaks that could create hazards or cause machine malfunctions.
    Once the vacuum pump has been switched on, it may only be switched off using the automatic functions provided for this purpose. Pressing the reset button or the emergency stop mushroom button has no effect on the vacuum pump, which continues to operate. The pump can be forced off using the dedicated button on the “Additional Controls” page.
    During automatic procedures, before lifting the piece, a dedicated sensor checks the vacuum status. If an adequate vacuum level is not detected, the “vacuum failure” alarm appears. The pump remains active until the vacuum switch detects a correct value or until it is disabled from the “Additional Controls” page.

    WARNING!

    Loss of power to the vacuum pump causes the vacuum to drop rapidly, resulting in the material lifted by the suction cups falling.

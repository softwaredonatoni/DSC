    DOC1 INTRODUCTION

    General information

    The information contained in this document applies only to the software versions specified on the title page.

    Some of the functions available in the product may not be described in this documentation; in such cases, Donatoni Macchine S.r.l. is under no obligation to guarantee these functions or retain them in future versions.

    Purpose of the manual

    This manual provides the operating instructions required to understand the D-Plant program correctly, as well as useful information for troubleshooting.
    Compliance with the instructions provided in this manual is essential to ensure correct operation, long service life and cost-effective use of the machine.
    The machine operation and maintenance manual and this software manual must always be available at the plant installation site, kept in a safe and easily accessible place. If either manual is lost, contact the Health and Safety Manager.
    Failure to comply with the instructions provided in this manual, negligence, or incorrect and improper use of the machine may cause the Manufacturer to void the machine warranty and may prevent the machine from delivering the benefits for which it was designed.
    DONATONI MACCHINE SRL remains available to provide prompt and accurate technical assistance and anything else that may help ensure optimum machine operation and performance.
    The Manufacturer reserves the right to modify this publication and the information it contains at its discretion whenever deemed necessary for the technical or commercial improvement of the product.

    Storing the manual

    This Software Manual must be kept throughout the machine's entire operating life, including if the machine is transferred to a third party.
    To store the Software Manual correctly, it is recommended to:

    • Use the Manual in a way that does not damage all or part of its contents; in particular, do not leave the Manual unattended during use and return it to its designated location immediately after consultation;

    • Do not remove, tear out or rewrite any part of the Manual for any reason;

    • Keep the Manual in an area protected from moisture, heat and other environmental agents that could affect its integrity or service life;

    • If all or part of this Manual is lost or damaged, request a copy of the documentation from the Manufacturer.

    Users

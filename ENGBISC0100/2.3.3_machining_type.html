    DOC2.3.3 MACHINING TYPE

    Machining can be performed in two modes:

    • Cutting

    • Sweeping

    Cutting

    In Cutting mode, parallel cuts following the selected profile are performed. The following parameters define their step, extension and direction.

    Parameters

    Step

    Indicates the distance between the position of one cut and the adjacent cut. If it is greater than the blade thickness, material remains between the passes.

    Stock allowance

    Thickness of material to be left above the required final section.

    Start/end extension

    Extends the start or end of the cut by the set value.

    Auto Increase

    When enabled, automatically increases the extension value according to the calculation performed by the system.

    Cut direction

    Defines the direction in which the cuts are performed, from right to left or from left to right, according to the selected configuration.

    Sweeping

    In Sweeping mode, the movement is transverse to the profile direction and is used to finish the workpiece surface.

    Parameters

    Sweep step

    Indicates the distance between one transverse movement and the next along the profile direction.

    Stock allowance

    Thickness of material to be left above the required final section.
